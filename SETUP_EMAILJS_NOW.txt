═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  HOW TO MAKE YOUR CONTACT FORM SEND EMAILS TO YOUR GMAIL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

⚠️ YOUR FORM IS NOT SENDING EMAILS YET BECAUSE YOU NEED TO:

1. SET UP EMAILJS (FREE - Takes 5 minutes)
2. REPLACE 3 VALUES IN YOUR CODE

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 1: CREATE EMAILJS ACCOUNT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Go to: https://www.emailjs.com/
2. Click "Sign Up" (it's FREE - 200 emails/month)
3. Sign up with your Gmail: foodfindr27@gmail.com

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2: CONNECT YOUR GMAIL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. After logging in, click "Email Services" (left menu)
2. Click "Add New Service"
3. Choose "Gmail"
4. Click "Connect Account"
5. Sign in with: foodfindr27@gmail.com
6. Click "Allow" to authorize EmailJS
7. Give it a name like "FoodieFindr Gmail"
8. Click "Create Service"
9. ⭐ COPY THE SERVICE ID (looks like: service_abc123xyz)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3: CREATE EMAIL TEMPLATE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Click "Email Templates" (left menu)
2. Click "Create New Template"
3. Fill in:

   Template Name: Contact Form
   
   To Email: foodfindr27@gmail.com
   
   From Name: FoodieFindr Contact Form
   
   Subject: New Contact Form Message from {{from_name}}
   
   Content (copy this exactly):
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────
   New message from FoodieFindr contact form:
   
   Name: {{from_name}}
   Email: {{from_email}}
   
   Message:
   {{message}}
   
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────
   This message was sent from your website.
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────

4. Click "Save"
5. ⭐ COPY THE TEMPLATE ID (looks like: template_xyz789abc)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 4: GET YOUR PUBLIC KEY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Click "Account" (top right) → "General"
2. Scroll down to "API Keys"
3. ⭐ COPY YOUR PUBLIC KEY (looks like: abc123xyz789)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 5: UPDATE YOUR CODE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Open: src/Contact Us.html

Find line 198 and replace:
   emailjs.init("YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY_HERE");
   
   WITH:
   emailjs.init("PASTE_YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY_HERE");

Find line 268-270 and replace:
   const serviceId = "YOUR_SERVICE_ID_HERE";
   const templateId = "YOUR_TEMPLATE_ID_HERE";
   const publicKey = "YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY_HERE";
   
   WITH:
   const serviceId = "PASTE_YOUR_SERVICE_ID_HERE";
   const templateId = "PASTE_YOUR_TEMPLATE_ID_HERE";
   const publicKey = "PASTE_YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY_HERE";

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 6: TEST IT!
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Open your Contact Us page
2. Fill out the form
3. Click "Send Message"
4. Check your Gmail inbox: foodfindr27@gmail.com
5. You should receive the message! ✅

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
NEED HELP?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

If it still doesn't work:
1. Press F12 in your browser
2. Go to "Console" tab
3. Try sending the form again
4. Check what error message appears
5. Make sure all 3 values are replaced (no "YOUR_..._HERE" left)
